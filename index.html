FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about bookkeeping services in Calgary.

How much does bookkeeping cost in Calgary?
We operate on a flat monthly fee model — you know exactly what you'll pay before we begin. Engagements start at $125/month and scale based on volume and complexity. Sole proprietor packages from $200/month, corporation packages from $425/month. We'll provide a custom quote after a free consultation.
What types of businesses do you work with?
We work with sole proprietors and corporations including contractors, consultants, restaurants, cafes, salons, real estate agents, e-commerce sellers (Shopify, Square, Stripe), daycares, auto repair shops, landscapers, wellness clinics, yoga studios, and more. Whether you're just starting or managing a growing operation, we tailor our services to fit.
What's the difference between catch-up and clean-up bookkeeping?
Catch-up bookkeeping gets you up to date if you've fallen behind — months of missing records. Clean-up bookkeeping fixes errors in existing books that may be disorganized or inaccurate. We offer both during onboarding or as a one-time service.
Is everything handled remotely?
Yes! Castle is fully digital. We use QuickBooks Online and Xero with automated reminders and secure document uploads. Manage everything from home, office, or while traveling. We serve all of Calgary, surrounding areas like Airdrie, Cochrane, Okotoks, and across Alberta.
Do you require a contract?
No. All services are month-to-month with automatic renewal. No long-term contracts, no surprises. Pause or cancel anytime with 30 days' notice. We believe great bookkeeping should earn your trust every month.
Do you serve businesses outside Calgary?
Yes! We serve businesses across Alberta including Edmonton, Red Deer, Lethbridge, Medicine Hat, and all rural communities. Everything is cloud-based, so location is never a barrier.
