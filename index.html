FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know about working with Castle.

How much does bookkeeping cost in Calgary? +
Flat monthly fee, and you know what you pay before we start. Starter packages typically begin at $125/month, Growth at $299/month. These are typical starting points. Book a quick call and we'll quote your exact needs.
What types of businesses do you work with? +
Sole proprietors, corporations, contractors, restaurants, salons, real estate agents, e-commerce sellers (Shopify, Square, Stripe), daycares, auto repair, landscapers, trades, consultants, wellness clinics, and more across Calgary and Alberta.
Can you help if my books are months behind? +
Absolutely. Catch up is one of our specialties. Whether you're 3 months or 3 years behind, we organize and reconcile everything. Most catch-ups are completed in 2 to 4 weeks. Old receipts. Missed months. No panic.
Do you require a contract? +
All services are month-to-month. Cancel anytime with 30 days' notice. No long-term contracts, no surprises. We earn your trust every month.
Is everything handled remotely? +
Yes. We use QuickBooks Online and Xero with automated reminders and secure uploads. We serve Calgary, Airdrie, Cochrane, Okotoks, Chestermere, Edmonton, Red Deer, and all of Alberta remotely. Stay in control without leaving your desk.
What software do you use? +
QuickBooks Online and Xero. We're certified in both. Cloud based, so you can access your books 24/7 from any device, anywhere.
How is Castle different from a CPA firm? +
We deliver professional quality with a personal touch, not a corporate price tag. Flat pricing, personal service, and we explain things in plain English. You'll know your bookkeeper by name, and they'll know your business.
Can you handle my GST and payroll? +
Yes. We handle quarterly GST filings, payroll processing, T4 slips, ROEs, and CRA remittances. Everything is included in our Growth and Scale packages, or available as add-ons.
Do you offer rush bookkeeping? +
Yes. If you have a tight deadline, a loan application, or a CRA letter that needs immediate attention, we offer rush bookkeeping services. Most rush projects are completed within 48 to 72 hours. Just let us know your timeline when you book your call.
