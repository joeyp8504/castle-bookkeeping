FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about bookkeeping services in Calgary.

How much does bookkeeping cost in Calgary?
Our monthly bookkeeping packages start at $299/month for small businesses in Calgary. Pricing depends on your transaction volume, number of accounts, and complexity. We offer a free consultation to provide an accurate, no-obligation quote tailored to your business.
What bookkeeping software do you use?
We work with QuickBooks Online, Xero, and Wave. We're certified QuickBooks ProAdvisors. If you're already using a platform, we'll work within it. If you're starting fresh, we'll recommend the best fit for your business and set it up for you.
Do you serve businesses outside Calgary?
Yes! We serve businesses across Alberta including Edmonton, Red Deer, Lethbridge, Medicine Hat, and rural communities. Everything is done remotely via cloud-based tools, so location is never a barrier. Many of our clients are Alberta-based but operate nationally.
Can you help if my books are months behind?
Absolutely — catch-up bookkeeping is one of our specialties. Whether you're 3 months or 3 years behind, we'll organize and reconcile everything. Most catch-ups are completed within 2-4 weeks. Starting at $499 depending on the backlog size.
Are you a certified bookkeeper?
Yes, our team includes Certified Professional Bookkeepers (CPB) and QuickBooks ProAdvisors. We're members of the Institute of Professional Bookkeepers of Canada (IPBC) and all work is supervised by a CPA. We follow all CRA compliance standards and maintain professional liability insurance.
How quickly can you start?
Most new clients are onboarded within 48 hours of signing up. We'll connect to your bank feeds, set up your chart of accounts, and start processing within the first week. If you need catch-up work, that begins immediately in parallel.
