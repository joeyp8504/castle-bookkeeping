Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 18, 2026

Castle Bookkeeping ("we", "us", "our") operates the website bookwithcastle.com and provides bookkeeping, tax preparation, payroll, and related financial services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Contact information: name, email address, phone number, mailing address
  • Business information: business name, type, revenue, number of employees
  • Financial records: bank statements, receipts, invoices, tax documents provided for bookkeeping services
  • Communication records: emails, text messages, phone call records related to service delivery

Information Collected Automatically

  • Website usage: pages visited, time on site, referral source, browser type, device information
  • Cookies: we use cookies and similar technologies for analytics and advertising (see Section 5)
  • IP address: collected for security and analytics purposes

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide bookkeeping, tax, payroll, and advisory services
  • To communicate with you about your account and services
  • To send appointment reminders and service updates
  • To process payments and invoices
  • To comply with CRA and other regulatory requirements
  • To improve our website and services
  • To run advertising campaigns (Google Ads, Facebook/Meta Ads) and measure their effectiveness

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Service providers: QuickBooks Online (QBO), Xero, payment processors, and cloud hosting providers who assist in delivering our services
  • Government authorities: CRA, provincial tax authorities, as required by law
  • Advertising platforms: Google and Meta/Facebook receive anonymized conversion data to optimize ad campaigns
  • Professional advisors: your accountant or CPA, with your authorization

4. Data Security

We protect your information using:

  • Encrypted cloud-based platforms (QuickBooks Online, Xero)
  • Secure document upload portals
  • Two-factor authentication on all accounts
  • Regular security audits and access reviews
  • Staff confidentiality agreements

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

5. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses:

  • Essential cookies: required for site functionality
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site
  • Advertising cookies: Google Ads and Meta/Facebook Pixel to measure ad performance and serve relevant ads

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect site functionality.

6. Your Rights

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA), you have the right to:

  • Access your personal information we hold
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Withdraw consent for certain uses of your information
  • Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal retention requirements)

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@bookwithcastle.com or call (587) 872-0602.

7. Data Retention

We retain your financial records for a minimum of 7 years as required by CRA. Contact and communication records are retained for the duration of our business relationship plus 2 years. You may request deletion of non-legally-required data at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email or by posting a notice on our website.
