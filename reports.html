Quarterly Insights
Q4 was your strongest quarter of the year. Revenue was up 12% compared to Q3, driven primarily by a surge in kitchen renovation projects.
Your profit margin improved steadily from 26.6% in October to 28.6% in December. This tells us you're getting better at estimating jobs and controlling material costs.
