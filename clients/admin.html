Discovery Call

Run a 10-15 minute discovery call to qualify a lead and gather pricing info. Fill in details as you go.

Lead Information
Call Timer
00:00
0 min10 min (sweet spot)15 min (wrap up)
Call Guide — Follow This Script
1
Opener (1 min)
"Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out. I'm Joey from Castle Bookkeeping. I saw you're looking for monthly bookkeeping — I'd love to learn a bit about your business to see if we're a good fit. This should only take about 10 minutes. Sound good?"
2
Business Overview (2 min)
"Tell me a bit about what your company does."
"How many employees do you have?"
"Where are you based? Any multiple locations?"
3
Current Bookkeeping (2 min)
"How are you handling your bookkeeping right now?"
"What software are you using, if any?"
"Are your books up to date, or do you need catch-up work?"
4
Scope & Volume (3 min)
"How many bank and credit card accounts does the business use?"
"Roughly how many transactions per month would you say? Ballpark is fine."
"Do you need payroll done? How many employees on payroll?"
"Do you need GST/HST filing?"
5
Pain Points (1 min)
"What's your biggest frustration with bookkeeping right now? What made you reach out?"
6
Quote & Close (2 min)
"Based on what you've told me, I can put together a quote for you. Typically for a business like yours, you'd be looking at around $[see pricing grid below] per month. That includes [list inclusions]. I'll send you a formal quote by email — does that work?"
