Welcome to Castle Bookkeeping

Welcome to Castle Bookkeeping, Shalini. As part of your onboarding, your first month of bookkeeping is on us. Once we receive your documents, we will categorize and reconcile your books, and deliver a clean monthly report in plain English. Along with your report, we will provide a quote for monthly bookkeeping moving forward. Payroll is not included in the complimentary month but will be quoted separately.

Documents

What We Need From You

Document Checklist

To get started on your complimentary month, please gather the following documents. You can upload them in the section below.

Bank Statements

  • All business bank account statements for the month
  • Credit card statements (if applicable)

Revenue Records

  • Parent payment records or fee schedule
  • Government subsidy documentation (if receiving provincial childcare subsidies)
  • Any grant funding documentation

Expense Records

  • Receipts for supplies, food, and snacks
  • Rent or mortgage payment documentation
  • Insurance payments
  • Utility bills tied to the daycare

Payroll (if applicable)

  • Employee pay records
  • T4 information or contractor payments
