Tax Optimization

Are You Claiming Everything?

An Airbnb expense checklist to make sure nothing is missed. These are all potentially deductible against your rental income.

Vehicle & Travel

  • ✓ Mileage to and from properties (guest check-ins, maintenance, cleaning supply runs)
  • ✓ CRA rate: $0.70/km for first 5,000 km, $0.64/km after that (2025 rates)
  • ✓ Use MileIQ or similar app for automatic mileage tracking
  • ✓ Parking fees at supply stores, hardware stores

Property Expenses

  • ✓ Mortgage interest (not principal) on rental properties
  • ✓ Property taxes (proportional to Airbnb use)
  • ✓ Property insurance
  • ✓ Utilities: gas, electric, water, internet, cable
  • ✓ Condo fees or HOA (if applicable)

Guest & Turnover

  • ✓ Cleaning service fees (between guests)
  • ✓ Laundry (sheets, towels, detergent)
  • ✓ Welcome supplies (coffee, snacks, toiletries)
  • ✓ Airbnb platform fees and service charges
  • ✓ Key lockbox, smart lock batteries, replacement keys

Maintenance & Furnishing

  • ✓ Repairs and maintenance (plumbing, electrical, HVAC)
  • ✓ Furniture, appliances, decor (capital cost allowance / depreciation)
  • ✓ Landscaping and snow removal
  • ✓ Professional photography for listings
  • ✓ Software subscriptions (pricing tools, channel managers, accounting)
Pro Tip: Track Your Mileage

Download MileIQ on your phone. It automatically tracks every drive and lets you classify trips as business or personal with one swipe. At CRA's rate of $0.70/km, a 30 km round trip to your Cochrane property is a $21 deduction every time. Over a year, mileage alone can add up to thousands in deductions.
