Year-End Bookkeeping Checklist for Calgary Businesses

March 6, 2026

The end of your fiscal year is a critical time for your Calgary business's finances. Proper year-end procedures ensure accurate financial statements, smooth tax filing, and a clean start to the new year. Here's your complete checklist.

Reconciliation

  • Reconcile all bank accounts through the last day of the fiscal year
  • Reconcile all credit-card accounts
  • Reconcile any loan accounts and verify outstanding balances
  • Reconcile accounts receivable — follow up on outstanding invoices
  • Reconcile accounts payable — ensure all bills are recorded

Review and Adjust

  • Review your chart of accounts for any needed cleanup
  • Verify all transactions are categorised correctly
  • Record any accrued expenses (bills received after year-end for the prior period)
  • Record depreciation entries for fixed assets
  • Write off any uncollectible receivables
  • Review and adjust prepaid expenses
  • Perform an inventory count if applicable

Payroll Year-End

  • Process final payroll for the fiscal year
  • Verify all source deductions were remitted correctly
  • Prepare T4 slips for all employees (due February 28)
  • Prepare T4A slips for contractors paid over $500
  • Issue any outstanding ROEs

GST/HST

  • File any outstanding GST returns
  • Reconcile GST collected versus GST remitted for the year
  • Review Input Tax Credits to ensure all eligible ITCs were claimed

Financial Statements

  • Prepare year-end income statement (profit and loss)
  • Prepare year-end balance sheet
  • Compare actuals to budget and prior year
  • Prepare any management reports needed for planning

Tax Preparation

  • Organise all tax documents and receipts
  • Deliver year-end financial package to your accountant
  • Discuss any year-end tax planning opportunities (RRSP contributions, equipment purchases, etc.)
