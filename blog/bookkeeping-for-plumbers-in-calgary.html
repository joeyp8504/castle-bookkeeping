Bookkeeping for Plumbers in Calgary

February 7, 2026

Running a plumber business in Calgary is rewarding, but managing the financial side can feel overwhelming. Plumbers face specific bookkeeping challenges including emergency-call revenue, parts inventory, and warranty-service tracking. Without a system in place, these complexities can lead to costly errors, missed deductions, and stressful CRA interactions.

Bookkeeping Challenges Unique to Plumbers

Every industry has its own financial quirks, and plumbers are no exception. The most common issues we see include:

  • Cash-flow management. Understanding when money comes in and goes out, and planning for lean periods.
  • Expense categorisation. emergency-call revenue, parts inventory, and warranty-service tracking. Getting categories right is essential for accurate tax filing.
  • GST compliance. Alberta businesses earning over $30,000 must register for and remit GST. Late or incorrect filings attract penalties and interest from CRA.
  • Payroll obligations. Source deductions, T4 preparation, and ROEs must be handled correctly to avoid CRA penalties.

Pro Tip for Calgary Plumbers

Track emergency versus scheduled-service revenue separately. Emergency calls typically carry higher margins.

Common Deductions for Plumbers

Calgary-based plumbers are often entitled to deductions for tools, vehicle costs, pipe and fitting inventory, and licensing fees. However, claiming these correctly requires proper documentation and categorisation. Our team ensures you capture every eligible deduction while maintaining CRA-compliant records.
