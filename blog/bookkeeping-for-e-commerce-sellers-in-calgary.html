Bookkeeping for E-Commerce Sellers in Calgary

March 1, 2026

Running a e-commerce seller business in Calgary is rewarding — but managing the financial side can feel overwhelming. E-Commerce Sellers face specific bookkeeping challenges including multi-platform sales tracking, shipping costs, inventory valuation, and cross-border transactions. Without a system in place, these complexities can lead to costly errors, missed deductions, and stressful CRA interactions.

Bookkeeping Challenges Unique to E-Commerce Sellers

Every industry has its own financial quirks, and e-commerce sellers are no exception. The most common issues we see include:

  • Cash-flow management — understanding when money comes in and goes out, and planning for lean periods.
  • Expense categorisation — multi-platform sales tracking, shipping costs, inventory valuation, and cross-border transactions. Getting categories right is essential for accurate tax filing.
  • GST compliance — Alberta businesses earning over $30,000 must register for and remit GST. Late or incorrect filings attract penalties and interest from CRA.
  • Payroll obligations — source deductions, T4 preparation, and ROEs must be handled correctly to avoid CRA penalties.

Pro Tip for Calgary E-Commerce Sellers

Reconcile each sales channel (Shopify, Amazon, Etsy) monthly so marketplace fees and refunds are accurately captured.

Common Deductions for E-Commerce Sellers

Calgary-based e-commerce sellers are often entitled to deductions for shipping supplies, platform fees, product inventory, and warehouse or storage costs. However, claiming these correctly requires proper documentation and categorisation. Our team ensures you capture every eligible deduction while maintaining CRA-compliant records.
