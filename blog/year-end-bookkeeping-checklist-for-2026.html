Year-End Bookkeeping Checklist for 2026

May 19, 2026

As 2026 draws to a close, Calgary business owners need to wrap up their finances properly. A thorough year-end process ensures accurate financial statements, a smooth tax-filing experience, and a clean slate for the new year. Here is your complete 2026 year-end bookkeeping checklist.

December Action Items

  • Record all revenue through December 31 — don't let late-December invoices slip into January
  • Enter all outstanding bills and expenses for December
  • Process your final payroll for the year
  • Make any planned year-end purchases (equipment, supplies) to capture 2026 deductions
  • Review your RRSP contribution room if you're a sole proprietor (deadline is March 1, 2027)

Reconciliation Checklist

  • Reconcile all bank accounts through December 31
  • Reconcile all credit-card accounts through December 31
  • Reconcile loan balances against lender statements
  • Review accounts receivable — write off any uncollectible amounts
  • Confirm accounts payable is complete — all December bills entered

Adjusting Entries

  • Record depreciation for all capital assets
  • Adjust prepaid expenses (insurance, rent paid in advance)
  • Accrue any expenses incurred but not yet invoiced
  • If applicable, perform a physical inventory count and adjust your records

Payroll and T4s

  • Verify all 2026 payroll has been processed
  • Confirm source deductions match CRA records
  • Prepare T4 slips — due by February 28, 2027
  • Prepare T4A slips for contractors if applicable

GST

  • File any outstanding quarterly returns
  • If you file annually, prepare your 2026 annual return (due March 31, 2027 for calendar year-end)
  • Reconcile total GST collected against GST remitted for the year

Financial Reporting

  • Generate year-end income statement and balance sheet
  • Compare 2026 results to 2025 and to your budget
  • Prepare your financial package for your accountant
